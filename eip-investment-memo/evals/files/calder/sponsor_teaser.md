# Halstead Ridge Partners — Project Cascade
## Confidential Investment Teaser (excerpt)

**Target:** A leading provider of water and wastewater infrastructure services to
municipal utilities in the Mid-Atlantic and Midwest.

### Investment highlights

- **Mission-critical, non-discretionary spend.** The majority of the Company's revenue is
  generated from municipalities operating under active EPA consent decrees, where
  remediation spending is mandated rather than elected.
- **Entrenched customer relationships.** Average tenure across the top five customers
  exceeds nine years, with a near-perfect renewal record.
- **Differentiated technical capability.** In-house condition assessment and hydraulic
  modelling capability that generates a proprietary pipeline into higher-margin
  rehabilitation work.
- **Significant runway.** A fragmented regional market with an identified pipeline of
  tuck-in acquisition opportunities and a proven greenfield yard expansion playbook.
- **Founder committed.** The founder-CEO is rolling meaningful equity and will continue
  to lead the business post-transaction.

### Transaction

Halstead Ridge is seeking a senior debt facility to support the acquisition. Indicative
enterprise value of approximately $78 million. Sponsor equity contribution of
approximately 55% of total capitalisation.
