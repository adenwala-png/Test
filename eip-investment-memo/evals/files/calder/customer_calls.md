# Calder Water Infrastructure — Customer Reference Calls

Conducted 18–26 March 2026 by EIP (A. Rivera). Scores are the reference's strength of
endorsement on a 1–5 scale, assigned by EIP.

---

## Call 1 — City of Akron, Ohio

**Contact:** Terrence Ballard, Deputy Director of Public Utilities
**Relationship:** 16 years | **FY2025 revenue:** ~$14.8mm | **Score: 4.5**

Ballard has worked with Calder since 2010, through three utility directors. Describes
them as "the only contractor we've never had to put on a corrective action plan."

Key points:
- Akron's consent decree runs through 2032 with roughly $310mm of remaining committed
  spend across all contractors. Calder holds the lining and inspection scope.
- Calder was rebid in 2023 and retained the MSA. Ballard said two national firms bid
  lower on unit price but "we scored them down on schedule risk and local crew depth."
- Asked about weaknesses: Ballard said mobilisation on emergency work "used to be a
  problem, maybe three years ago" but has improved since Calder based more equipment in
  Akron permanently.
- Asked whether a change of ownership concerns him: "If the crews stay, I don't care who
  owns it. If the crews go, we have a problem."

## Call 2 — Pennsylvania American Water

**Contact:** Susan Okonjo, Manager, Capital Delivery
**Relationship:** 9 years | **FY2025 revenue:** ~$10.1mm | **Score: 4.0**

- Calder is one of three approved lining contractors on PAW's Pennsylvania footprint.
  Okonjo said Calder holds "roughly half" of the awarded volume, allocated by
  availability and past performance rather than a fixed split.
- Praised the condition assessment work specifically: "Their modelling group is better
  than the engineering firms we've used for the same scope, and cheaper."
- Concern raised: PAW is centralising procurement across states in 2026–27 and Okonjo
  could not say whether the approved-contractor list would be re-run. "It's above me.
  I'd guess they grandfather existing performers, but I wouldn't promise it."
- **EIP note: this is an unresolved open item. Flagged for further diligence.**

## Call 3 — Henrico County, Virginia

**Contact:** Bill Trask, Utilities Engineering Manager
**Relationship:** 6 years | **FY2025 revenue:** ~$8.6mm | **Score: 4.0**

- Henrico awarded Calder an on-call MSA in 2020, renewed 2025 through 2030.
- Trask said pricing is "not the cheapest, usually middle of three" but that Calder's
  reporting and data deliverables are why they renewed: "We can hand their condition
  data straight to our asset management system. Nobody else does that without us
  reformatting it."
- Volume grew from ~$3mm in FY2021 to ~$8.6mm in FY2025 as Henrico expanded its
  rehabilitation program.

## Call 4 — City of Lancaster, Pennsylvania (former customer)

**Contact:** Dale Fenner, former Public Works Director (retired 2024)
**Relationship:** 2017–2022, contract not renewed | **Score: 2.5**

EIP sought this call specifically to understand a lost MSA.

- Lancaster did not renew Calder's MSA in 2022. Fenner said the decision was price:
  "They came in about 18% above the winner on the rebid and they didn't sharpen it."
- Fenner was complimentary on quality — "no complaints about the work" — but said Calder
  "had gotten comfortable" and did not treat the rebid as competitive.
- Noted Calder's Harrisburg crews were stretched at the time; he believed capacity
  constraints drove the pricing.
- **EIP note: management characterised historical MSA losses as two in fourteen years but
  had not identified which. This is one of them. The second remains unidentified.**

## Call 5 — Regional engineering firm (channel partner)

**Contact:** Anonymised at request. Principal, mid-Atlantic water practice.
**Score: 3.5**

- Specifies Calder on roughly a third of the rehabilitation projects they design.
- Said the trenchless lining market in the mid-Atlantic is "getting more crowded — two
  national roll-ups have bought regional players in the last eighteen months and they're
  bidding aggressively to fill crews."
- Believed Calder's assessment/modelling capability is a genuine differentiator that the
  roll-ups have not replicated.
