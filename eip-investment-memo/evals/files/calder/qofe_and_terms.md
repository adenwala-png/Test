### Other QofE observations

- Revenue recognition is percentage-of-completion on project work, as-performed on MSA
  task orders. CBIZ found no material issues.
- Net working capital averaged 11.2% of revenue over the trailing 24 months. DSO of
  62 days, elevated versus peers, driven by municipal payment cycles.
- CBIZ flagged that **backlog has not been independently verified** — the company's
  backlog figure includes MSA task orders not yet issued. CBIZ recommends the diligence
  team obtain a contracted-only backlog figure.

of **$78.0 million**, or **6.7x** FY2025 adjusted EBITDA of $11.66 million.

## Sponsor — Halstead Ridge Partners

Founded 2019, based in Chicago. $640mm AUM across two funds. Focus on lower-middle-market
infrastructure services businesses with $5–20mm EBITDA. Fund II closed at $410mm in 2024.

Lead partner Tomás Nakamura previously led two water-sector platforms at a predecessor
firm. Halstead Ridge has completed six platform investments and eleven add-ons to date;
two platforms have been realised, at reported gross multiples of 2.4x and 3.1x.

Ridgeline Capital is a family office based in Minneapolis providing co-investment equity.
EIP has not previously transacted with Ridgeline.
